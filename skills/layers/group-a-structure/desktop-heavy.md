# Structural Focus: Desktop-Heavy

When generating a desktop-heavy structure:

1.  **Grid:** Utilize expansive 12 or 16-column grids.
2.  **Navigation:** Favor persistent sidebars or massive, multi-column mega-menus over hidden hamburger menus.
3.  **Density:** Data density can be increased. Utilize horizontal space for complex tables, side-by-side comparison panels, and multi-pane layouts.
4.  **Hover States:** heavily rely on complex hover interactions (tooltips, expanding rows) which are native to mouse pointers.
