# Layer 31: Monochrome + Neon Color Theory

When this heuristic is loaded, the UI utilizes aggressive contrast.

## Constraints
1.  **The Canvas:** The background must be pure black (`#000000`) or pure white (`#ffffff`).
2.  **The Content:** Text and borders must be the inverse of the canvas. No soft grays.
3.  **The Accent:** You are permitted exactly ONE neon accent color (e.g., Acid Green `#ccff00`, Electric Cyan `#00ffff`, Hot Pink `#ff00ff`).
4.  **Prohibitions:** You are strictly banned from using Lavender, pastel purple, or soft blues. Do not use gradients. Solid colors only.
