# Design School: Carbon Design System

You are operating under the Carbon constraint.

## Principles
1.  **Rationality:** Every component must have a clear, mathematical reason for existing.
2.  **Grid:** Strictly adhere to a 16-column grid structure.
3.  **Typography:** IBM Plex Sans. Use weight (Light, Regular, SemiBold) to establish hierarchy rather than purely relying on size differences.
4.  **Color:** Utilize the core Carbon Gray palette for UI structures, with Blue (`#0f62fe`) reserved strictly for primary actions.
