The State-Reset Architecture

Group A: Structure

Layers 1-10 constrain the grid. Forcing masonry, asymmetric tension, and avoiding basic center alignment.

Group B: Typography

Layers 11-20 dictate type scales. Banning intermediate font weights, forcing negative tracking on massive headers.

Group C: Components

Layers 21-30 define geometric constraints. 0px Brutalism or 9999px Pill radii. No more generic 8px squircular slop.

Group D: Aesthetics

Layers 31-40 control light and color. Forcing pure flat design, heavy glassmorphism, or high-contrast brutalist gradients.

Group E: Physics

Layers 41-50 govern motion and Z-space. Spring physics, scroll-driven animations, and WebGL immersive layers.
